The game

The lane battles a generation grew up with on Flash, made deeper and built to play with friends.

Stay in Your Lane is a side-scrolling strategy game. Build your economy, upgrade your base, and send waves of Gym Rats, Holy Cows and High Horses down one lane until the enemy base falls. Play the story campaign and puzzle challenges, or go online in 1v1 and team up with a friend in 2v2.

The demo is out on Steam, and the release date is open.

Trailer and screenshots

What makes it different

  • The lane battles people grew up with, made deeper. Base and building upgrades, choosing how many units go in each wave, awakenings that change your plan, and gold miners that keep comebacks possible.
  • Play with friends. Online 1v1 and 2v2, co-op against the CPU, and 8 match mods that change how a battle plays.
  • A full package for a small price. Story campaign, puzzle challenges and 5 CPU difficulties, at about $9.

Where the game is

Done (in the demo)

  • 2 factions: The Herd and Verdant Grove
  • 13 units, 6 spells, turrets and landmines
  • Tech tree per faction, unit awakenings
  • Campaign opening with story and tutorial
  • Puzzle challenges with hints
  • Building upgrades: base, gold mine and turrets
  • Wave queueing and an army size that grows with your base
  • Stickers to send in online matches
  • Leaderboards
