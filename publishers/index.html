Info for publishers

A side-scrolling real-time strategy game. Mine gold, unlock units, cast spells and push down one lane until the enemy base falls. Solo, vs CPU, or online 1v1 and 2v2.

Where the game is

Done (in the demo)

  • 2 factions: The Herd and Verdant Grove
  • 13 units, 6 spells, turrets and landmines
  • Tech tree per faction, unit awakenings
  • Campaign opening with story and tutorial
  • Puzzle challenges
  • Online 1v1 and 2v2, Steam and Discord invites
  • CPU opponents on 3 difficulties, 2 maps
  • Free browser version on itch.io

Coming in the full game

  • 4 to 5 factions
  • About 40 campaign levels
  • About 30 challenges
  • Build your own faction tree
  • Ranked online battles

The campaign system is finished, so new levels mainly need story writing. An artist is already working on the new factions.
