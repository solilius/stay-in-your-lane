How a match plays

You can't steer your units. Once they're out, they march. Your job is everything else.

  1. 1

    Mine gold

    Send miners to the gold mine. More miners, more units. Fewer miners, faster attack.

  2. 2

    Unlock new units

    Spend gold on your tech tree to unlock units, spells and upgrades in the order you want.

  3. 3

    Cast spells

    Use mana to root, heal, shield or blast at the moment the fight turns.

  4. 4

    Build defenses

    Turrets and landmines buy time when the other side pushes too hard.

  5. 5

    Bring their base down

    Win the lane, keep pushing and knock the enemy base flat.

Pick your side

Every unit, spell and upgrade is an animal pun. Every pun can win you the match.
